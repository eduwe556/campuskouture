About CampusKouture

Our Mission

CampusKouture is a fashion marketplace created specifically for university students in Nigeria. We bring together trusted local vendors onto one platform so you can discover affordable, stylish clothing without spending hours moving from market to market.

We believe that looking good shouldn't be stressful or expensive. By connecting you with curated campus vendors, we make it easier to find outfits that fit your budget and your style.

How It Works

1. Browse our collection of clothes, shoes, bags, and accessories from multiple vendors.

2. Click on any product to see details and place an order using the simple form.

3. We contact you to confirm payment and delivery. Then your order is prepared by the vendor and delivered to you on campus.
